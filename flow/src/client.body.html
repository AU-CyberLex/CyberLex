What you can do

  • Read doctrine and sit the three-question drill in each module.
  • Attach a lesson link from YouTube, Vimeo, Drive or a direct file.
  • Run the six lab benches and three branching investigations.
  • Review case cards, collect points and badges, save a local score.
  • Export progress as JSON, import it on another device, or clear it.
What you cannot do. There is no sign-in, no cloud sync, no public profile and no way to see another learner's device data.

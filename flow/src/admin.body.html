Administrator view · local operations

Operate the studio. Without inventing a backend.

The admin view is an operations console for a static product: video-registry health, the device-local progress file, gamification rules and the role/permission matrix. There are no accounts to manage and no learner records on a server, because Phase-1 has neither.

Sandbox and privacy

  • Every iframe carries a sandbox attribute.
  • Referrer policy is strict-origin-when-cross-origin.
  • No cookies, analytics or third-party scripts are loaded by the app shell.
  • A generic https URL is framed as-is; faculty should use a known provider.
  • Clearing the slot deletes the local override, not the source video.

What is real, what is seeded

The UI, scoring rules, badge logic, export/import format, video normalisation and sandbox policy mirror the Phase-1 app. The learner name, cohort rows and some scores are seeded so the demo has something to show on first open. Reset the demo from the top bar at any time.

No accounts in Phase-1. Role pages are scopes for the demo, not an authentication system. A real deployment has no server-side learner record, so there is no cohort analytics or cross-device sync.

Scope boundary

  • In P7: 12 case cards, progress reset/export/import, video slots, points/badges/local leaderboard, demo email, role-scope doc.
  • Out of P7: statutory copy changes, lab scoring fixes, sim runner hardening, shell/router/theme, staging deploy.
  • No server: no accounts, no cohort analytics, no cross-device sync.
  • No PII: display names and scores stay in the browser.

Five-minute demo script

0 of 8

What to say

1
“P7 is four surfaces, one state.”

Cases, progress, videos and gamification all read from the same local progress record.

2
“The learner owns the data.”

No account, no server. Export is the portability story.

3
“The empty state is designed.”

A missing video is not a broken iframe; it is an attach prompt.

4
“Points cannot be farmed.”

Only the saved score counts; re-answering does not add points.

5
“Roles are scopes, not auth.”

Phase-1 has no accounts; the role pages demonstrate boundaries.

Ask for. A yes/no on P7 scope, a note on whether the video fixtures are acceptable for the demo, and a decision on whether Phase-2 needs a class code for real cohort analytics.
